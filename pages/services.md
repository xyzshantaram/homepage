## Included

- Development costs
- All source code and assets that are created during the development process, and relevant documentation
- The price will include the initial design and two free major revisions. Major revisions include things like redoing entire sections of a website, changing design and structure on a large scale, etc. Additional revisions are chargeable.

## Not included

- Third-party service costs - Things like domain, hosting, external APIs - you pay these directly to the respective service provider.

## Notes

- Prices are subject to change. **[Enquire now](/contact)** to confirm.
- 1 free hour of consultation is included, regardless of whether we decide to work together
- While my standard pricing for international clients is **$35/hour**, if you're working toward a good cause or a non-commercial project, feel free to reach out, and we can find a rate that works for both of us.
- I accept payments via **PayPal** as well as **XMR**, and **UPI** for Indian customers.
