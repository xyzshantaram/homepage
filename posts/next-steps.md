## Next Steps

Next Steps is an experiment in using generative AI in a non-chat form. You write
down a goal and whatever thoughts you have about it, press a button, and get one
answer: a readback, one concrete next step, five possibilities, and a closing
line. There is no back-and-forth.

It is part of Thursday. The setup screen offers a free 24-hour key from AI For
All, or you can point it at your own OpenAI-compatible provider. The prompt is
deliberately concrete: every step has to name a place, a call, a file, a number
of minutes, or a person. It is not allowed to say "research your options" or
"make a plan".

The thing I like about it is the shape. Most AI products are trying to keep you
in the conversation. This one is trying to get you out of the tab and into the
world. The closing line is the only place where it is allowed to be warm.
