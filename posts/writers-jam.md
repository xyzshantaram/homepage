Writers Jam was a writing exercise I started in 2025 and continued
for the better part of a year.

The premise was simple: get some of my friends together, write 100
words on a theme every week, and give them a place to publish it.

Writers Jam received 367 posts totalling 110,168 views between
2025 and 2026.
